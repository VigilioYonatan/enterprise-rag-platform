# Backup scope, encryption and offsite responsibilities

The scripts in `platform-ops/scripts` implement an encrypted **logical** PostgreSQL backup with an offsite rsync transport and an isolated restore drill. They do not capture raw MinIO objects and are **not PITR**. Do not approve production data recovery until two independent backups exist (PostgreSQL + MinIO), a consistent snapshot/restore plan is proven, WAL archiving is configured with pgBackRest or WAL-G to an independent offsite destination, and target RPO/RTO are timed and recorded. Backups may include sensitive data; retain encryption keys entirely outside the source repository. Use an unprivileged backup identity in production rather than PostgreSQL superuser. No scheduled execution, successful restoration, or offsite transfer has been observed here.
